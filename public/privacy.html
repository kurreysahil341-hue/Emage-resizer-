Privacy Policy — ParikshaSnap

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Developer: Sahil Kurrey (sahilkurrey441@gmail.com)

1. 100% Offline Processing (No Data Collection)

ParikshaSnap processes all photos, signatures, thumb impressions, and documents strictly locally on your device using HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared with any external server, cloud storage, or third party.

2. Device Storage & Gallery Access

When you choose an image, ParikshaSnap uses your device's standard system file picker. When you tap Download / Save, the processed image or PDF is saved only to your device's local Gallery album (ParikshaSnap) or Documents/ParikshaSnap folder.

3. No Ads, No Analytics, No Tracking

ParikshaSnap contains zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, and requires no user account or login.

For questions or support regarding this Privacy Policy, contact: sahilkurrey441@gmail.com.
